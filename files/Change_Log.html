CHANGE LOG

What changed in the rules and tools.

23 Nov 2026

  • Fight Simulator (including the live claude.ai version): nerfed Hyrein's Cubification. Below Level 4, the cubed body part is now left on the field instead of just vanishing -- if its original owner reaches it on their own turn before Hyrein does, they restore it themselves (spending that turn to do it). Reclaim (Level 4+) still lets Hyrein alone absorb the cube from range as a free action, permanently closing that window, so this only changes Level 2-3 play; it doesn't move the Level 5 tier list, where Reclaim always wins the race.
  • Fight Simulator (including the live claude.ai version): Aftokrator characters now carry the Aftokrator Ward instead of a Border Shield (Block +3, escalating Trion cost simplified to a flat cost), and Galopoula characters now carry the free, no-Trion-cost Galopoula Shield (Block +2) — neither was implemented before, which was quietly inflating Border's win rate in every tier-list run.
  • Fight Simulator: fixed a stat-budget bug affecting Hyrein, Viza, Gatlin, Wen Sō, Ratarikov, Reghindetz and Koskero's premade builds. Every other premade spends a full six-point ability-score array (+3, +2, +1, +1, 0, -1); these seven were quietly shortchanged, most fielding only four of those six points and none reaching +3 in their own weapon's stat.
  • Fight Simulator: Viza, Reghindetz and Koskero got a Lethal and/or reach bump, and Wen Sō's Decoy no longer costs a Trion every single round just to stay projected (only once now) — all were still clearly under the field average after the stat fix. Sniper and Shooter got a small accuracy bump after slipping into the bottom tier once the other seven classes' stats were corrected. Viza and Reghindetz turned out to need more than a numbers patch: fight logs showed them losing almost every matchup against ranged/control-heavy opponents, since as reach-1/4 melee they couldn't close the distance before being chipped apart. Both are now mid-range fighters instead of close-range — Organon's rings and Thugater's whip reach 50 ft (matching Ratarikov), their proximity-based features scale with that new reach, and Organon's Signature Gear got one more accuracy/damage bump on top. Both are now much closer to the field average.
  • Gatlin's Four Arms: bigger Block per arm, an arm only breaks when its Block is actually beaten (not just used), and arm Blocks can now stack past the usual +6 cap. New Level 2 feature, Overlapping Guard: commit several arms to one Block at once for a bigger bonus, at the cost of breaking all of them together if it still fails.
  • Borrowed Orders (Level 7, every class) is now spelled out in full everywhere it's listed instead of "see above"/"see below".
  • Border, Aftokrator and Galopoula technology is now explicit in the rules as nation-exclusive: Border sub triggers (Shield, Kogetsu, Scorpion, and the rest) only work for Border classes. Aftokrator gets its own sub trigger, the Aftokrator Ward (an escalating-cost, no-frills Block). The Galopoula Shield is reframed as Galopoula's own tech rather than "Border-style".
  • Banners added for all 10 Aftokrator and Galopoula classes, matching the Handbook's class banners.
  • Border Table Online, Character Creator Offline, and the Fight Simulator (including the live claude.ai versions) now support all 10 Aftokrator/Galopoula classes, with their Signature Gear visible as real weapon stat rows in the Character Creator.
  • Enemy Cards: fixed three Part 4 boss cards (Ranbanein, Hyrein, Viza) that still listed pre-redesign abilities.
  • Fight Simulator (including the live claude.ai version): Hyrein, Viza, Gatlin, Wen Sō, Ratarikov, Reghindetz and Koskero now simulate their full Level 1-10 progression instead of fighting at Level 1 Signature Gear stats only, including Cubification/Reclaim/Divide the Line, Organon Rings/Collapsing Shield/Full Expansion, Four Arms/Overlapping Guard/Cannon Mount, Decoy/Misdirection/Which One, Circling Blades/Combine/Full Circle, Focus Strike/Overextend/Reach Guard/Full Charge, and Black Wall/Full Spiral. Also fixed a bug where Viza's and Wen Sō's Signature Gear rolled against a 0-point ability score, leaving their to-hit bonus near zero regardless of Level.

22 Nov 2026

  • Feedback page: a form on the site that emails feedback straight to the maker.
  • Visitor cookie notice added site-wide. With consent, the site now counts visits and roughly where they come from (country, city, network address) in a private, owner-only database. A separate owner-only analytics page shows the numbers, including a view that flags when the same network address shows up under more than one visitor ID.
  • Border Table Online: the table's owner can now see a player's password (passphrase known only to the owner). Players are told this on the join screen and asked to pick a password they do not use anywhere else.
  • Border Table Online: personal codes are gone. Every player makes their own password the first time they join, and types it again next time. The Operator cannot read it, only reset it (Reset password).

21 Nov 2026

  • Dark mode button on every page.
  • Character maker: search box to find and add weapons and sub triggers.
  • Border Table Online: print and download buttons on character sheets.
  • Excel character sheet with formulas (Level Bonus, Max Trion with the free first weapon, Evade, wound limits).

20 Nov 2026

  • First weapon is free of Trion cost (2 per extra weapon, 1 per sub trigger).
  • Character maker: sample characters for all nine classes, level-up helper, print and download/load buttons, Main menu button, clickable Handbook page links on every row.
  • Books: Main menu link in every footer, clickable page numbers, page numbers in indexes.
  • Quick reference card, enemy cards and a starter adventure added.
  • Border Table Online: picture import for maps and tokens, map library with folders, maps fill the board, Operator password change, art and blue theme.
  • Focused Shield now gives -1 Lethal on the attack it blocks.

Earlier

  • Player’s Handbook (48 pages) and Operator Guide (76 pages) in Word and PDF.
  • Wounds and Trion became one pool. Every class has Basic Guard.
  • Border Table Online (no accounts, peer-to-peer).
