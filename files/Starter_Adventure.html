STARTER ADVENTURE: THE PARKING LOT GATE

A first mission for three Level 1 agents.

Draft and untested. For 3 players at Level 1 (about 135 combined Trion). Operator runs it with Border Table Online or at a real table. Expected length: one session.

The hook

A small Gate opens at the edge of a quiet district. Border HQ sends a three-agent team to close it before a Neighbor patrol gets out. Nobody can be hurt for real: Trion bodies bail out, they do not die.

Scene 1: The briefing (10 minutes)

  • Introduce the agents. Each player says one sentence on why they joined the Border.
  • Give them a goal: reach the Gate, find out what came through, shut it down.
  • Tell them where the Gate is: an empty parking lot behind a school. Warn them about Bamsters.

Scene 2: The patrol (fight 1)

Four Minions (18 Trion each) sweep the lot. Use cover (half cover, -2 to hit). Players get a surprise round if they approach Hidden: no reactions for the enemy, +2 to hit against them.

Scene 3: The gate guard (fight 2)

One Soldier (about 50 Trion) and one Skitter (9 Trion) guard the Gate. Evade 10, +3 to hit, +1 Lethal. The Soldier retreats when it falls to 10 Trion, which is a chance for the Operator to talk about Neighbors.

Total enemy Trion across both fights: about 131, close to the players’ combined Trion, as the Handbook recommends.

Scene 4: Closing the Gate

  • An INT or Trap Sense character can find the Gate’s anchor; a TRION character can overload it.
  • Success: the Gate closes. Reward: each agent earns one Link point to start the next mission with 2.
  • If someone bails out, they wake up in the Border base and can still help from the Operator room.

Tips for the Operator

  • Roll to hit, roll where, roll how bad, read the table, update Trion. Repeat.
  • Use the quick reference card and the enemy cards.
  • In Border Table Online: import a map picture in the Operator tab, add tokens, and drag them around.
