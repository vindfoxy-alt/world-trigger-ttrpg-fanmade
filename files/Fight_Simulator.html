Trion Fight Simulator

Pick two teams (or bring your own agent) and watch the fighters move across the map and fight round by round, or run a couple of thousand fights to see who wins more often. It uses your own rules: d20 + stat vs Evade, Block, hit locations, wounds, Nearly Dead and bail out.

Everyone in the fight, enemies included, uses this Tier: Tier Bonus on attacks and Evade, and Trion per Tier.

This is a rough model. Agents imported from a character code now use their class features for their Tier, their special ability and their sub triggers (listed below), so Tier 2 and higher results are closer to the real rules. The premade fighters in the dropdowns are now full characters built the same way (a class, a special ability, weapons and sub triggers, shown as chips on their cards), so they follow the same rules as imported agents. Tick Turn-based to choose who the Blue team attacks on each turn. Enemies scale with the Fight tier (Minion, Soldier and Elite gain Trion per Tier; Bosses and Black Triggers get Trion per player). Imported agents use their real stats, first weapon and Trion. Block adds up: Basic Guard +2, plus +2 for Kogetsu or Raygust, plus +2 if an imported agent carries a Shield (it costs 2 Trion each time it is used, 1 for a Counterstriker), up to +6. Fighters move up to 30 ft (5 ft squares), then attack. Melee reaches 5 ft, ranged fighters use their weapon range and need a clear line of sight. Not simulated: Link points and Team Plays, flanking, surprise and noticing, ability checks, Attacker Quick Trigger, Sword Master and Mantis, Gunner Overwatch, Ace Gunner, Shooter Composite and Area Control, Keep Them Moving, Called Shot targeting beyond the chest, Trapper Web Weaver and Entangle, Ground Strike, Assassin Chameleon Approach, Decoy and Break Their Formation, Tactician Control the Space, Exploit a Habit and Rapid Reassessment, Counterstriker Reprisal, Bulwark, Hold the Line, Lampyris and Vorvoros Tier features, Borrowed Fire, Perfect Parallel Processing, Wall Breaker, Trap Sense, the Shield techniques, and the sub triggers Radar, Bagworm Tag, Enhanced Radar, Silencer and Timer (they have no effect in this simulator). Tier 5 specialisations are fixed to the first option. Chelidon has Gun Swap (rifle, shotgun or sidearm each attack), the Sidearm Block, Jet Pack and Strafing Run; the rules give Strafing Run no limit and it wins fights alone, so the simulator allows it once every 6 rounds. Enemy groups use the Monster Manual creatures: Skitter, Hollow Blade, Lancer, Shade Stalker, Glider, Bulwark, Gatecrawler, Mirrorwarden, Bamster, Bado, the three Dogs, Marmod, Idra, Ilgar and the Rabbit, plus the newer warriors Rad (Gate Drain and Gates), Spilco (clings, then explodes; the target can pry it off), Vander (locks on, then fires a beam; a big hit to its eye cancels it, shown as 10 or more Trion lost), Pneura, Arugda (drains Trion and heals), Lilith (learns Block against each attacker), Gieve with Charon (Edge die) and the Pergrande (three beams, and a Giant Bomb every third round), with their main traits (simplified: Rads hiding inside other warriors, Pergrande's underside weak spot, passing as a civilian beyond starting Hidden, Arugda sizes, Lancer's Laser Sight, Guard Dog's Cover Pack, Idra's Remote Control, Radar and the Rabbit's variants are not modelled). To keep fights hard as your Tier rises, the enemy group gets extra members at higher Tiers and bosses gain Trion per Tier (see the numbers in the notes below). Lampyris has Piercing Shards (Tier 2), Marker Shards (Tier 4: allies get an Edge die against a marked target), Spotter (Tier 5: hidden enemies within 60 ft are seen) and the Tier 10 Shield; it always fires its Guns form, so Shield (before Tier 10) and Wings are not used. Vorvoros has Liquefy (twice a round at Tier 10, the second costs 2 more Trion), Gas Form (Tier 4, used when low on Trion; before Tier 8 it stops only the first attack) and Choking Cloud (Tier 5, used when it catches 2 or more enemies). Resist Heat is not modelled because the simulator has no fire. Borrowed Fire (Tier 7, once a fight, no Link cost) lends an ally the matching feature for a round. Escudo, Switchbox, Dummy Beacon, Idaten, Teleporter, Grasshopper, Chameleon, Bagworm and Geist are simplified. Black Triggers: a fighter marked + Black Trigger starts ignited (Edge die on attacks, Lethal +1, Block +3, lose 2 Trion a round). The Black Trigger bosses use the Monster Manual numbers with their powers simplified. Balance in this simulator: Attacker, Assassin and Counterstriker move 40 ft, Counterstriker and Assassin get +1 to hit, Lampyris Shard Guns are +0 to hit with Lethal 2 and ignore cover, Liquefy costs 2 Trion and Escudo gives Block +3. Map and movement rules are my first draft, built from your 30 ft move and weapon ranges, and are untested at the table. Treat the win rates as a sanity check on balance, not a prediction.
